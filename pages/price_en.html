Courses & costs

Prices

All prices are total prices per person including VAT. They are made up of the daily rate × course days, the training room (€10 per course day) and, for SDI/TDI, the certification (€35). If you need more time, each additional day is charged at the same daily rate. At most three students per course, and I match up individual sign-ups.
