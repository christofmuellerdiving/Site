My focus

You practise unclipping and clipping cylinders and gas switches mid-water until you can do them without losing trim or your place in the team.

TDI Sidemount Diver is for divers who want to use sidemount in technical diving as well. With the certification you may dive sidemount with safety and decompression stops, as far as your other certifications allow. Compared with the SDI course it adds more propulsion techniques, unclipping and clipping cylinders while hovering, and gas switches with and without a mask.

The two days

We meet at 8:30 at Deepstop in Schwetzingen. Each day starts with theory, then we set up equipment and practise every procedure on land before we dive in a lake nearby. Every dive ends with a debrief. The standard requires at least two days and three open water dives with a total bottom time of at least 90 minutes; four hours of classroom and briefings are suggested.

Theory

You learn to match gas within the team, including cylinders of different sizes, and to manage gas in two independent cylinders. On equipment we talk about cylinders, regulators, sidemount system, weighting and configuration. Communication is by hand and light signals. Problem solving covers gas sharing, major gas loss, light failure, loss of visibility, entanglement and self-rescue. We also cover tight spaces, conservation, difficult entries, stress and the sidemount S-drill.

In the water

Before every dive we run START and talk through what could cause stress underwater and how you deal with it. You dive with frog kick, modified frog kick, flutter kick and back kick, and turn on the spot with a helicopter turn. You hover without moving hands or feet and hold your position on the descent, on the bottom and on the ascent. Mid-water you unclip and clip your cylinders and switch gas with and without a mask. Along the way you keep track of your gas, look back regularly so you recognise the way out, and deploy a lift bag. You may carry additional cylinders on the course, but they are optional.

Standards and certification

Trim, buoyancy and team diving are held to the same standard as in my GUE courses. Certification is through TDI. All dives take place in open water. I don’t offer TDI Advanced Sidemount, which is designed for overhead environments such as caves and wrecks. For open water sidemount, I offer this course, Intro to Tech and personal training.

Combinations and prerequisites

The course can be combined with Advanced Nitrox, with at least four dives in total, and with Decompression Procedures, in which case the standards of both courses apply. You need SDI Open Water Scuba Diver or equivalent and a minimum age of 18. The course fits if you already do technical dives or want to, including if you come from GUE.
