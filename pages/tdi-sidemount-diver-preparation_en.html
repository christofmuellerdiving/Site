Preparation

TDI Sidemount Diver

Before every dive you run through START: S-drill, Team, Air, Route, Tables. For the course you do the same, just a few days earlier. This page lists everything the course asks of you.

What you need for the course

Structured by START

If you're missing something, we can usually lend it to you.

Before the start

Paperwork

  • Diver Medical Participant Questionnaire PDFFill it in before the first session, including before the theory. If you answer yes to question 3, 5 or 10, or to any question on page 2, you need a doctor's clearance on page 3, so leave time for that. Please don't send it by email or through the contact form. Bring it filled in to the first session.
  • Liability Release PDFFor reading ahead. You sign it at the first session before we start, together with everyone in the class.

Prerequisites: SDI Open Water Scuba Diver or equivalent, minimum age 18.

  • Certification cards and logbook as proof of the prerequisites
S

S-drill

Regulators

  • Two DIN regulator sets: one on a 2.1 m long hose, one on a short hose on a necklace, each with a pressure gauge
  • Inflator hoses for wing and drysuit
  • Three hose retainers
T

Team

What your buddy sees in the check

  • Sidemount system with inflator and a pouch for spares
  • Weights, a few sizes for fine-tuning
  • Mask and backup mask
  • Open-heel fins with a stiff blade, no split fins
  • Cutting tool

Spares for the pouch

  • Spare bungee
  • Spare double ender
  • Spare mouthpiece
  • Zip ties

Suit and thermal protection

In a wetsuit you need neoprene boots, and the base layer, socks and p-valve kit stay at home.

  • Drysuit and undersuit, or a wetsuit, suited to the water temperature and the length of the dives
  • Base layer
  • Warm socks, for example from Fourth Element
  • Hood
  • Gloves, wet and dry, plus liner gloves
  • For the p-valve: condom catheters with click adapter, or She-P or Dive-P, plus adhesive and remover
A

Air

Cylinders

Additional cylinders are optional in this course.

  • Two sidemount cylinders
  • Analyser and tools, if you have them
R

Route

Entry, exit and the way underwater

  • Compass, a computer with a compass is fine
  • Spool with at least 30 m of line, and a buoy

Lights

  • Primary light with Goodman handle, battery pack and charger
  • Backup lights and spare batteries
T

Tables

Depth and time

  • Dive computer and a second device for depth and time, ideally a second computer
  • Wet notes with pencil

After the dive

At the water

  • Two beanies, one tends to go missing
  • Something to eat and drink
  • Personal medication
