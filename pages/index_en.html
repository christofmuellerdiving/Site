GUE · Global Underwater Explorers

GUE courses

GUE trains divers to uniform standards for equipment, procedures and teamwork, from the first dive into technical diving. Because equipment and procedures are the same worldwide, GUE divers can dive together as a team without long briefings on how they do things. I have taught GUE courses since 2018, from Performance Diver through Fundamentals, Advanced Open Water Diver and primers to DPV 1.

How training works

Open water, not pool

Dives take place in local lakes such as Rheinauer See or St. Leon. Courses normally run from March to November.

Your gear gets fitted

Before the first dive I check your equipment and adjust it to you. Missing items can be rented.

Land, water, debrief

Expectations

Who my courses suit

A good fit

  • You want to dive better, not just collect another certification card.
  • You want honest feedback. On GUE courses I grade every skill on a scale of 1 to 5 and go through each one with you.
  • You like learning in a small group of at most three students, in open water rather than a pool.
  • You don't have a regular dive buddy. I match up individual sign-ups.
  • If something is still missing, you add days at the same day rate.

Not a good fit

  • You expect a guaranteed certification. You are certified only once you meet the requirements of the standard.
  • You want to learn in a swimming pool. I teach by “train like you fight”: cold, a drysuit, thick gloves and poor visibility belong in training, because that is how you will dive later. Master it in a lake and warm water feels easy.
  • You smoke and want to take a GUE course. Being a non-smoker is a prerequisite in the GUE standard.
  • You have no dive accident insurance. GUE courses require insurance including hyperbaric treatment, for example DAN at Master level.
