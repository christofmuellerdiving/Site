GUE · Global Underwater Explorers

GUE courses

GUE trains divers to uniform standards for equipment, procedures and teamwork, from the first dive into technical diving. Because equipment and procedures are the same worldwide, GUE divers can dive together as a team without long briefings on how they do things. I have taught GUE courses since 2018, from Performance Diver through Fundamentals, Advanced Open Water Diver and primers to DPV 1.

Why learn with me

Teaching is my profession

Diving well is not enough. Anyone who teaches has to spot mistakes before they turn into habits and be able to explain how to fix them.

Self-employed trainer for over 25 years

I train staff at industrial companies and public authorities, where only what sticks counts. Participants rate every session on a feedback form, and a client only books again if their people can do afterwards what they were meant to learn. My clients have booked me regularly for years. I teach at the lake the same way: every procedure is broken into steps, and we rehearse every skill on land before we get into the water. I teach the theory in person, too. More sticks in conversation than with online learning alone, because you can ask questions straight away.

GUE instructor since 2018

Nobody becomes a GUE instructor overnight. The prerequisites are your own technical training with GUE and plenty of tech or cave diving experience outside of courses. Those who are accepted have to prove themselves in several stages before different examiners, physically as well. The final test is a complete course that the candidate runs alone while a GUE examiner assesses them.

On this path I learned to teach skills such as trim and buoyancy so that everyone learns and is assessed against the same standard.

Expectations

Who my courses suit

A good fit
  • You want to dive better, not just collect another certification card.
  • You want honest feedback. On GUE courses I grade every skill on a scale of 1 to 5 and go through each one with you.
  • You like learning in a small group of at most three students.
  • You don't have a regular dive buddy. I match up individual sign-ups.
  • If something is still missing, you add days at the same day rate.

Not a good fit
  • You expect a guaranteed certification. You are certified only once you meet the requirements of the standard.
  • You want to learn in a swimming pool. I teach by “train like you fight”: cold, a drysuit, thick gloves and poor visibility belong in training, because that is how you will dive later. Master it in a lake and warm water feels easy.
  • You smoke and want to take a GUE course. Being a non-smoker is a prerequisite in the GUE standard.
  • You have no dive accident insurance. GUE courses require insurance including hyperbaric treatment, for example DAN at Master level.
