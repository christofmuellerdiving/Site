My focus

Cause first, drill second. What looks like a trim problem on video often comes down to weight or cylinder choice.

Not every goal needs a new course. In personal training we work on specific skills. The basis is a video analysis of your dives: we look at where things go wrong, and I show you how to fix them. We agree on the content beforehand.

Price: half day €100, full day €225, plus gas and lake entry.
