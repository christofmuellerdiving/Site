My focus

Sidemount starts with cylinder choice and weight distribution. We set up your rig so you sit horizontal before we work on skills.

SDI Sidemount Diver is the sidemount course for recreational divers. You learn to dive with two independent cylinders at your sides and manage the gas in both, including when something goes wrong underwater. All dives stay within no-decompression limits, to 40 m or the limit of your certification, whichever is shallower.

The two days

We meet at 8:30 at Deepstop in Schwetzingen. Each day starts with theory; how much depends on what you already know. Then we set up your equipment and walk through every skill on land. After that we drive to a lake nearby for one or two dives, and the day ends with a debrief. The standard requires at least two open water dives, each with a full briefing and debriefing.

What you learn

Theory covers gas management with independent cylinders and the equipment: cylinders, regulators, sidemount system, weighting and how it all fits together. We talk about hand signals, gas sharing and what to do when a regulator free-flows. Different entry techniques and the sidemount S-drill are part of it too.

You plan every dive yourself, including surface interval and no-decompression limit, and keep track of depth, time and gas consumption on your slate. You practise clipping on the cylinders out of the water, standing in the shallows, at the surface out of your depth and at depth. You also switch between regulators, work on buoyancy and make safety stops.

Standards and certification

Trim, buoyancy and team diving are held to the same standard as in my GUE courses. Certification is through SDI. You are certified once you meet everything the standard requires. If something is still missing, you add more days at the same day rate.

Afterwards

With the certification you may dive sidemount without direct supervision, with safety stops and within the limits of your other certifications. For sidemount on decompression dives, there is TDI Sidemount Diver. If you want to move into technical diving, the next course is TDI Intro to Tech, which I also teach in sidemount.

Prerequisites

SDI Open Water Scuba Diver or equivalent, and a minimum age of 18, or 15 with parental consent. If you are not sure sidemount is for you, try it first on a try dive.
