Preparation

GUE Navigation Primer

If the backup mask or the medical is missing on the day, that time comes out of our time in the water. So this page lists everything the course asks of you. It follows the GUE EDGE you run through before every dive. Once everything is ticked off, the course starts with the first briefing.

What you need for the course

Structured by GUE EDGE

If you're missing something, we can usually lend it to you.

G

Goal

Before the course

Prerequisites: GUE Open Water Diver, Performance Diver or Fundamentals, then 15 non-training dives.

  • Theory from the GUE course material, which arrives after registration
  • Certification cards and logbook as proof of the prerequisites
U

Unified Team

Fit and registered

  • GUE registrationThrough your profile on gue.com. The other forms you fill in via DocuSign, and the email for it arrives shortly before the course.

GUE requires you to be a nonsmoker. For prescription drugs, except birth control, and for any prior medical condition that may pose a risk while diving, you need a physician's written authorization beforehand.

  • Medical as proof of your fitness to dive
  • Insurance that covers diving emergencies such as hyperbaric treatment, for example DAN at Master level or equivalent
  • Swimwear and goggles for the swim test: 275 m without stopping in under 14 minutes, and 15 m underwater on a breath hold
  • Personal medication
E

Equipment

Dive gear

  • Backplate with a harness made of one continuous strap with buckle, a crotch strap and D‑rings on the left hip and both shoulders
  • Wing without bungees, sized for your cylinders
  • Weights and weight belt, a few sizes for fine-tuning
  • Low-volume mask and backup mask
  • Stiff, non-split fins
  • Wrist-mounted compass
  • Cutting tool
  • Wet notes with pencil

Lights

  • Primary light with Goodman handle, battery pack and charger
  • Backup light with alkaline batteries, not rechargeable

For the team

  • One primary reel or an additional spool per team
E

Exposure

Thermal protection

In a wetsuit you need neoprene boots, and the base layer, socks and p-valve kit stay at home. For a drysuit you need 15 prior non-training dives in one, or 10 after the Drysuit Primer.

  • Drysuit and undersuit, or a wetsuit, suited to the water temperature and the length of the dives
  • Base layer
  • Warm socks, for example from Fourth Element
  • Hood
  • Gloves, wet and dry, plus liner gloves
  • For the p-valve: condom catheters with click adapter, or She-P or Dive-P, plus adhesive and remover
D

Deco

Depth and time

  • Dive computer, or depth gauge and timer
  • Spool with at least 30 m of line, and a buoy
G

Gas

Cylinders and regulators

In doubles you need 15 prior non-training dives in doubles, or 10 after the Doubles Primer.

  • Single cylinder or double 12 l with an isolator manifold, ideally filled with nitrox 32
  • Regulators in GUE configuration: primary on a 1.5 to 2 m long hose, backup on a short hose on a necklace, analog pressure gauge, inflator hoses for wing and drysuit
  • Analyser and tools, if you have them
E

Environment

At the water

  • Two beanies, one tends to go missing
  • Towel
  • Something to eat and drink
